About Delhivery

Delhivery is an Indian logistics and supply chain services company that was founded in 2011. It is headquartered in Gurugram, Haryana, India. The company offers a wide range of products and services, including express parcel transportation, cross-border e-commerce solutions, fulfillment services, warehousing, and last-mile delivery.


It operates through its integrated network and technology platform, which enables efficient and seamless delivery services. Delhivery has a significant presence in the Indian market and operates across 2,500 cities and towns in the country. It serves various industries, including e-commerce, healthcare, automotive, consumer goods, and retail.


The company has established a strong network of fulfillment centers and delivery hubs to ensure prompt and reliable services to its customers. On a global scale, Delhivery has expanded its operations by making strategic investments and forming partnerships. It has made several acquisitions, including acquiring India's largest cross-border e-commerce logistics provider, Aramax, in 2018.


The company also has joint ventures and partnerships with international logistics firms to enhance its global reach and capabilities. Delhivery has gained a strong market position in the Indian logistics industry. It is one of the largest logistics and supply chain service providers in the country, with a substantial market share.


The company competes with other major players in the market, such as Blue Dart, FedEx, and DHL, but has managed to establish a strong foothold due to its extensive network, innovative technology, and customer-centric approach. In terms of major achievements, Delhivery has received recognition and awards for its contribution to the logistics industry and for its innovative solutions. The company has focused on investing in technology and automation to improve its efficiency and speed of delivery.


As of the latest known information, Delhivery is operating in a dynamic and rapidly growing market. It continues to expand its network and services to meet the increasing demand for logistics and supply chain solutions in India and globally. The company's strong market position, strategic partnerships, and focus on technology-driven solutions position it well for future growth and success
